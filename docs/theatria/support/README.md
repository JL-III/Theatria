# 🚩 Support

Need help? Here are some options:

* **Ask in Chat**: Our community and staff are here to help.
* **Explore the Wiki**: Browse detailed guides here in our wiki.
* **Join our Discord**: Connect with us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/jYS5rR2HxP) for updates and community questions.
  * Open a [staff support ticket](https://discord.gg/hFJWRDKyNz) if your issue requires staff assistance.
* **Email:** If you cannot access Discord, email [playtheatria@venture23.net](mailto:playtheatria@venture23.net). Email replies are generally slower.

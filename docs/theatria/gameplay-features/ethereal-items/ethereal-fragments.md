# Ethereal Fragments

Fragments are obtained within [meteorite](../../events-challenges/meteorites.md) treasure barrels.

After collecting 16 Ethereal Fragments, [open a staff support ticket](https://discord.gg/hFJWRDKyNz) to arrange the trade for an Ethereal Item.
